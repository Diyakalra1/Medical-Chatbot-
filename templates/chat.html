About MedAssist

MedAssist is a medical evidence copilot designed to provide grounded health information.

What it is

An evidence-grounded system that retrieves, reranks and validates medical knowledge before generating a response.

What it is not

A doctor, diagnostic system or replacement for professional medical care.
